## 01. Company overview [ORG-01]

Northstar Workspace is a fictional direct-to-customer workplace equipment company founded in 2019. Its mission is to make compact home offices comfortable, reliable, and easy to support. Its headquarters and primary support team are in Lahore, Pakistan. Fictional fulfillment partners operate in Lahore, Austin, Leeds, and Dubai. A fulfillment partner is not a customer-facing shop; customers cannot collect or return products there without an approved appointment.

Northstar Workspace sells keyboards, desk lighting, laptop accessories, connectivity equipment, and ergonomic desk products. Its catalog serves individual professionals, students, remote teams, and small businesses. The company has 86 fictional employees: 16 in customer support, 20 in operations, 18 in product, 14 in engineering, 8 in finance and compliance, and 10 in people operations and administration. Department counts describe this demonstration snapshot only.

Northstar Workspace accepts consumer delivery addresses in Pakistan, the United States, the United Kingdom, and the United Arab Emirates. It does not currently accept consumer orders for Canada, Australia, the European Union, or other destinations. Bulk business orders to an unsupported country require a separately approved written quote and cannot be promised by frontline support.

Northstar Workspace operates an online store only. There are no walk-in retail stores, repair counters, public warehouse collection desks, or telephone sales lines in this demonstration. A support agent must not invent a warehouse street address, a local branch, a telephone number, or a payment account. The exact legal registration number, tax registration numbers, bank details, and employee personal contact details are deliberately absent from this knowledge base.

## 10. Shipping regions, charges, and delivery estimates [SHIP-01]

Northstar normally dispatches stocked consumer products within two warehouse business days after payment acceptance. The daily processing cutoff is 14:00 in the dispatch warehouse's local time. Preorders and separately quoted business shipments use their written dates instead of this standard dispatch target. Inventory and dispatch dates must be checked in the operational record.

Standard shipping to ordinary supported addresses in Pakistan, the United States, the United Kingdom, and the United Arab Emirates normally takes three to five shipping business days after dispatch. Remote-area standard delivery normally takes five to eight shipping business days after dispatch. The delivery estimate is additional to processing time. A two-day processing target plus a three-to-five-day standard transit estimate is not a promise of arrival two days after checkout.

Express shipping, when offered at checkout, normally takes one to two shipping business days after dispatch in Pakistan, the United States, and the United Kingdom. United Arab Emirates express normally takes two to three shipping business days after dispatch. Express is unavailable for remote areas and unsupported postcodes. It does not remove the normal processing period. All transit figures are estimates, not guaranteed service levels.

Standard consumer shipping costs USD 6 for a paid merchandise subtotal below USD 100 and USD 0 for a paid merchandise subtotal of USD 100 or more. Orders may arrive in separate parcels. A delivered status for one parcel does not establish delivery of every item in a split order. ## 11. Delays, missing parcels, and wrong-item deliveries [SHIP-02]

Report the mismatch within seven calendar days of delivery. Northstar pays the approved corrective shipment and, when needed, supplies a prepaid return method. If tracking shows a parcel is being returned to sender, support checks the carrier reason before proposing another shipment. Additional charges or eligibility depend on the verified reason; an agent must not invent a universal redelivery fee. ## 12. Return eligibility and authorization [RET-01]

For a new, unopened, nonfaulty physical product, the customer must request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery. The packaging must remain factory-sealed, and the original included accessories must be present. For an opened, nonfaulty new product, the request window is 14 calendar days from delivery. The opened product must be clean, complete, undamaged, and suitable for resale. Opened nonfaulty new-product returns have a 15% deduction from the paid item price.

For a nonfaulty refurbished product such as Renew NK-100-R, the request window is 14 calendar days from delivery. A 10% deduction from the paid item price applies, whether the refurbished packaging has been opened or not. Refurbished goods do not inherit the new unopened product's 30-day change-of-mind window. Their listing's described cosmetic condition is not itself a manufacturing defect.

Confirmed manufacturing defects reported within the first 30 calendar days, confirmed transit damage, and Northstar fulfillment errors have no restocking deduction. A defect first reported after 30 days follows the product's warranty rather than automatically creating an unrestricted change-of-mind refund. Clearance marked final sale excludes change-of-mind returns, but does not cancel applicable defect remedies, a published warranty, or mandatory legal rights.

Return authorization is required before sending goods. The approved authorization identifies the item, return destination, deadline, and shipping method. Customers must not send goods to an address found through an unrelated web search or to a fulfillment warehouse without authorization. The customer has seven calendar days after the authorization is issued to hand the package to the approved carrier and retain proof of handover. A requested extension needs written support approval.

For a change-of-mind or wrong-size return, the customer pays return shipping unless a promotion or written approval says otherwise. For a confirmed manufacturing defect, transit damage, or Northstar fulfillment error, Northstar supplies or reimburses the specifically approved return method. Reimbursement of an unapproved premium courier is not guaranteed. Products must be packed securely, with accessories and any issued return identifier included.

Return requests outside the published window, incomplete packages, signs of misuse, disputed delivery dates, and unclear product condition require manual review. A policy document alone does not establish that a particular order is refundable; verify the operational record, item condition, dates, and any prior resolution.

## 13. Refund amounts, timing, and exceptions [RET-02]

An approved item refund is based on the actual paid item amount after allocated discounts, not the current price or original list price. Apply any valid restocking deduction to that paid item amount. Tax is adjusted only as required by the verified invoice and applicable tax treatment. Support must not fabricate a tax adjustment when the invoice or rules are unavailable. A bundled product uses its invoice allocation rather than its individual full list price.

For example, an opened nonfaulty new TypeWave purchased at the full USD 79 item price and returned within 14 days has a 15% restocking deduction of USD 11.85. Its item refund before any tax adjustment is USD 67.15. A nonfaulty Renew keyboard purchased for USD 55 has a 10% deduction of USD 5.50, leaving USD 49.50 before tax adjustment. These examples exclude shipping and do not describe a particular customer's live eligibility.

Original outbound shipping is not refunded for a change-of-mind return. If every item in an order is returned because of confirmed Northstar error or transit damage, the original outbound shipping charge is included in the approved resolution. For a partial fault return, Billing determines any eligible shipping allocation from the actual invoice. A free shipping charge of USD 0 does not create an additional cash shipping refund.

Returns are normally inspected within three business days after receipt at the authorized return center. After inspection and authorized approval, Northstar submits a refund within two business days. The issuing bank or payment provider may take a further five to ten business days to show the funds. These are separate stages. The agent must not promise that a refund appears in the bank account immediately after approval or immediately after the customer posts a parcel.

Refunds go to the original payment method. A request to refund to another person's card, a cryptocurrency wallet, a gift code, or an agent's bank account must be escalated to Billing and Security. ## 21. Troubleshooting playbooks [TECH-01]

### TypeWave NK-100: Bluetooth pairing or wrong key layout

For TypeWave pairing, confirm the keyboard is charged, switched on, and in Bluetooth mode rather than receiver mode. Remove an obsolete saved pairing entry on the computer and repeat the documented pairing procedure. Test within a short distance without a metal obstruction. If the receiver is being used, confirm the receiver is connected to the intended computer and that any required USB-C adapter supports data. Charging alone does not create a wired keyboard connection.

For swapped modifier keys, check TypeWave's physical Windows/macOS switch and the operating system's keyboard layout. Confirm the problem occurs in more than one application. Record the exact operating system and connection mode before escalating. Do not advise an undocumented factory-reset key combination or firmware download link. This knowledge base does not publish model-specific pairing shortcuts or downloadable firmware packages.

### AeroDock ND-800: no external display

For AeroDock with no HDMI picture, first confirm the laptop's exact USB-C port supports DisplayPort Alternate Mode. Then check monitor input selection, HDMI cable capability, a direct connection without an unverified adapter chain, and operating-system display settings. A data-only USB-C laptop port cannot produce video through this hub. A Link100 cable cannot supply USB-C video, even though it supports charging.

If the host supports video but flicker occurs, inspect the cable and target resolution, reduce the display mode as a diagnostic step, and check whether the host can sustain the requested output. Do not promise 4K 60 Hz without host and cable support. A dock becoming slightly warm in normal use is different from a burning smell, visible damage, or unsafe heat; the latter signs require stopping use and safety escalation.

### AeroDock ND-800: slow charging or network problems

For slow laptop charging through AeroDock, note the charger's wattage, the cable rating, and the laptop's required input. AeroDock may reserve up to 15 W. A Link65 source leaves at most 50 W for the laptop, and a 90 W laptop is not promised full-speed charging. Do not claim the hub creates extra power. If the laptop charges directly but not through the hub, record both results and the negotiated power information when the operating system exposes it.

For Ethernet problems, confirm the Ethernet cable is seated, the router port works, and the operating system sees a network adapter. Test a known working cable and distinguish no network link from an internet service outage. Gigabit link capability is not a guarantee of gigabit internet throughput. Support does not ask for a router password or recommend disabling all security controls as a permanent fix.

### GlowFlex NL-200: no light or flicker

For a GlowFlex lamp that will not turn on, confirm the supplied adapter, wall outlet, and barrel connector are seated and visually undamaged. Check the touch control and try a normal brightness setting. Test the outlet with an unrelated safe device if convenient. Do not substitute a random USB-C charger, open the adapter, or bypass its power supply. Stop immediately if there is exposed wiring, a burning smell, or heat damage.

For an intermittent lamp, record whether the symptom changes with normal control operation and whether the original adapter is used. A video may help only if it can be obtained without recreating a hazard. The published color range is 2700-6500 K; the presence of several color settings is normal. GlowFlex has no internal battery, so operation while unplugged is not expected.

### FocusCam NC-100: black image or missing microphone

For a black FocusCam image, open the physical privacy shutter, select FocusCam in the application, check operating-system camera permissions, and close another application that may hold the camera. Test a direct USB connection before adding a hub. The supported maximum is 1080p at 30 frames per second, not 4K. Lighting and network quality can affect a meeting even when the camera is functioning normally.

For a missing microphone, select the intended input device and check microphone permissions independently from camera permissions. Closing FocusCam's lens shutter does not establish that its microphones are muted. Record the meeting application, operating system, and whether the operating system's sound test detects input. Do not request an account password to change application settings.

### ClearCall NH-220 and QuietClick NM-210

For ClearCall audio issues, select the headset as output and input, inspect the inline mute control, inspect the application's mute state, and test another USB data port. ClearCall is wired and cannot be paired using Bluetooth. Its microphone noise reduction does not promise active noise cancellation in the earcups. A damaged cable or connector should be escalated rather than repaired by the customer.

For QuietClick pointer lag, use a fresh correctly fitted AA battery, confirm the selected wireless mode, move the receiver away from interference, and test a suitable mouse surface. Check the battery compartment for a stored receiver before treating it as missing. Do not plug a charging cable into the mouse; QuietClick is not rechargeable. Stop use if battery leakage or damaged contacts are present.

### Mechanical fit and maintenance

For an Orbit arm that drifts, verify the monitor weighs 2-9 kg without its original stand, the VESA pattern is compatible, and installation followed the supplied guide. Do not adjust a loaded arm in a way that allows the screen to fall. If the desk is glass, hollow-core without an approved reinforcement, or outside the 10-60 mm clamp range, the installation is unsupported. A compatibility review is required before recommending hardware changes.

For a sleeve fit issue, compare the laptop's complete external dimensions with Transit's internal 345 x 245 x 24 mm space, including any bulky case. For a new GlideMat that curls, allow up to 48 hours on a flat surface; do not heat it with an iron. For AirCool noise, disconnect it before inspecting surface dust or obvious damage. For DeskSense, confirm flat-monitor mechanical fit and an adequate 5 V, 2 A power source.

## 22. Support workflow, queues, and human approval [OPS-01]

The normal support sequence is: read the issue, identify the customer and relevant order, retrieve the applicable policy, inspect authorized order records, propose a response or action, obtain authorized human review, execute an approved action through the designated system, and record the outcome. Routine status and product questions normally have normal priority. A verified duplicate captured charge or a request with a documented urgent business impact should receive high priority for review. ## 24. Frequently asked questions [FAQ-01]

Can a USD 99 merchandise order get free standard shipping? Not under the USD 100 post-discount merchandise threshold. Tax or a DeskCare fee does not raise the merchandise subtotal for that calculation.

Can I stack WELCOME10 with Home Office Essentials? No, unless a separately approved campaign explicitly permits it. The normal WELCOME10 rules exclude bundles and allow only one promotional code.

Does a 100 W Link100 cable make Link65 a 100 W charger? No. Link65 remains a maximum 65 W source, and the attached device must support the negotiated power profile.

Will AeroDock display video from every USB-C port? No. The host port must support DisplayPort Alternate Mode. A data-only USB-C port cannot provide HDMI video through AeroDock.

Is Link100 suitable as a monitor cable? No. Its USB 2.0 data and charging specification does not include USB-C video or Thunderbolt support.

Can I use GlowFlex without its power adapter? No. GlowFlex has no internal battery and requires its supplied mains adapter.

Does TypeWave work as a wired keyboard when charging? No. Its USB-C connection charges it; typing uses Bluetooth or the wireless receiver.

Can QuietClick be recharged? No. QuietClick uses one replaceable AA battery and has no charging port. The battery is not included.

Does ClearCall provide active noise cancellation for the wearer? No. Its specified noise reduction is for the microphone, not active cancellation in the earcups.

Can an opened new nonfaulty product be returned after 20 days? Not under the standard 14-day opened-product change-of-mind policy. A defect or another applicable right requires its own review rather than an automatic rejection of every claim.

Can a support agent approve their own financial action just because the customer agrees? No. An authorized human reviewer must inspect the evidence and approve the financial or fulfillment action through the approved process.

Where can I find today's stock count or the current price in PKR? Not in this fixed knowledge document. Stock, live currency conversion, order status, processor events, and confirmed arrival dates require the appropriate current operational source.
